# Publishing Guardrails

The goal is a durable technical brand, not a spam machine.

## Approval Gates

Require explicit user approval before:

- Publishing to any external platform.
- Scheduling posts.
- Replying to comments or DMs.
- Posting in Reddit communities.
- Mentioning or tagging people/companies.
- Using paid promotion.
- Claiming monetization eligibility.
- Publishing anything based on private notes, emails, personal data, or local-only secrets.

## Originality

Acceptable AI assistance:

- Research support.
- Drafting.
- Editing.
- Summarization.
- Captioning.
- Repurposing Joe's source material.
- Generating thumbnail/social-card briefs.

Not acceptable:

- Fake personal stories.
- Fabricated results.
- Reused content presented as original.
- Engagement bait that is unrelated to Joe's work.
- Mass-produced platform spam.
- Hiding AI involvement when it materially shaped the artifact and disclosure is expected.

## Claims Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

- Does every technical claim map to a source, test, repo, or current doc?
- Are all platform rules current as of today's date?
- Are monetization claims based on official docs?
- Are screenshots and clips free of secrets, tokens, private paths, or private customer data?
- Are repo links public or intentionally private?
- Does the CTA match what exists now?
- Does the content teach something even without the link?

## Reddit-Specific Safety

- Read subreddit rules before drafting.
- Do not automate posting or replies.
- Do not repeatedly post the same link across subreddits.
- Prefer text-first posts that stand alone.
- Disclose affiliation when sharing Joe's own project.
- Ask for critique or share a lesson, not just "check this out."

Official references:

- Reddit spam policy: `https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam`
- Reddit content policy: `https://redditinc.com/policies/content-policy`

## Short-Video Safety

- Do not use copyrighted audio/video assets unless platform-native licensing clearly covers the usage.
- Avoid showing private terminal output, tokens, emails, or local customer data.
- Prefer original screen recordings, narrated demos, and generated diagrams.
- Verify platform-specific length, monetization, and originality rules before making production claims.

## Automation Boundary

Use AI and scripts to prepare:

- Drafts.
- Variants.
- Captions.
- Metadata.
- Upload checklists.
- Scheduling queues.

Do not let automation independently decide:

- What gets published.
- Where it gets posted.
- Who gets tagged.
- How comments are answered.
- Whether paid promotion runs.
